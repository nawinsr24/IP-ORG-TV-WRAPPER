Tamil TV

A simple TV-style player for Mac, Windows and Android

Educational project. Free, no ads, not monetised. This app does not host or own any content; it only plays public streams listed by the iptv-org project. Any legal concern about a channel or its content relates to the original stream source and the iptv-org repository. The app asks you to accept this notice each time it opens. Use it responsibly and lawfully in your country.

🤖 Android

Phones, tablets and Android TV. Allow “install unknown apps” for your browser when asked.

Controls

← →Previous / next channel
↑ ↓Volume up / down
0–9Type a channel number to jump to it
SStar / unstar a favourite
[ ]Previous / next favourite
LChannel list with search
CCycle category (news, movies, music, kids, religious…)
SpaceMute
TSleep timer
FFull screen (desktop)

Android phone: tap for controls, swipe left/right to change channel, swipe up/down for volume.

Many community streams are offline or geo-blocked, so some channels will be skipped.
